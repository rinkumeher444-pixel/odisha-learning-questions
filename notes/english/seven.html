ODISHA CLASS 10 • ENGLISH POETRY

WE ARE SEVEN

William Wordsworth

Childhood→Family→Death→Innocence
Central contrast
Adult’s understanding of death ↔ Child’s innocent understanding
Girl
Eight years old • lives with her mother
Key idea
She insists: “we are seven”
A. LEAD-IN

ପଢ଼ିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ

Children often see the world from a different perspective because of their simple nature. ଏହି poemରେ adult ଓ little childଙ୍କ death ବିଷୟରେ understanding କିପରି ଭିନ୍ନ, ତାହା ଦେଖାଯାଏ।

🎯 Learning Objective: ଶିଶୁର innocence ଓ adultର logical counting ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଥିବା contrastକୁ ବୁଝିବା।
B. LET’S LISTEN

କବିତା ଶୁଣିବା ଓ ପଢ଼ିବା

Teacher reads→Listen carefully→Read silently→Understand

ପ୍ରଥମେ teacher କବିତାକୁ aloud ପଢ଼ିବେ। ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟଥର ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ word ଓ line ଉପରେ ଧ୍ୟାନ ଦେବା। ପରେ silently ପଢ଼ିବା।

STANZA 1

ଶିଶୁର ସରଳତା

“A simple Child,
That lightly draws its breath,
And feels its life in every limb,
What should it know of death?”

Simple English: The poet presents a simple child full of life and asks what such a child should know about death.

ସହଜ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶିଶୁଟି ସରଳ ଏବଂ ଜୀବନରେ ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ। ତେଣୁ ଏପରି ଶିଶୁ death ବିଷୟରେ କେତେ ଜାଣିପାରିବ—କବି ପ୍ରଶ୍ନ କରୁଛନ୍ତି।

📌 Remember: First stanzaର focus — simple child, life ଓ death ବିଷୟରେ childର limited understanding.
THE GIRL

ଆଠ ବର୍ଷର Cottage Girl

The poet meets a little cottage girl.
She says she is eight years old.
Her hair is thick with many curls clustered around her head.
She has a rustic, woodland air and is wildly clad.
Her eyes are fair and her beauty makes the poet glad.
8 years old→Rustic girl→Simple appearance→Poet is glad
FAMILY COUNT

“How many may you be?” — “Seven in all”

At Conway
Two of them dwell.
Gone to sea
Two have gone to sea.
Church-yard
One sister + one brother lie there.
With mother
The little girl dwells near the graves.
2 Conway+2 sea+2 church-yard+girl=7
🧠 Girl's idea: The two dead children are still counted as part of the family. The poet, however, thinks they are no longer living.
THE ARGUMENT

Poet vs Little Maid

Poet’s view

Two are in the church-yard → they are dead → therefore only five remain.

Girl’s view

Two are in the church-yard → their graves are close → she still lives with them through memory and daily association → “we are seven.”

Poet: “only five”↔Girl: “we are seven”
THE GRAVES

ମୃତ ଭାଇ-ଭଉଣୀଙ୍କ ସହ ତାଙ୍କ ସମ୍ପର୍କ

The graves are green and can be seen twelve steps or more from her mother’s door.
The graves are side by side.
She often knits her stockings there.
She hems her kerchief there.
She sits on the ground and sings a song to them.
Often after sunset, when it is light and fair, she takes her little porringer and eats her supper there.
💡 ଏହି details ଦେଖାଏ ଯେ ଝିଅଟି ପାଇଁ church-yardରେ ଥିବା ଭାଇ-ଭଉଣୀ ତାଙ୍କ ଜୀବନରୁ ସମ୍ପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଅଲଗା ହୋଇନାହାନ୍ତି।
JANE AND JOHN

ଦୁଇ ମୃତ ଭାଇ-ଭଉଣୀଙ୍କ କଥା

Sister Jane

She lay moaning in bed until God released her of her pain. Then she went away and was laid in the church-yard.

Brother John

When the ground was white with snow, John died and was laid beside Jane.

Jane suffers→God releases her pain→Jane buried
Snowy ground→John dies→John lies beside Jane
ENDING

“O Master! we are seven.”

“But they are dead; those two are dead!
Their spirits are in heaven!”

“Nay, we are seven!”

Simple English: The poet repeatedly says that the two are dead, but the little maid refuses to change her answer.

ସହଜ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: କବି ଯୁକ୍ତି ଦେଇ କହୁଛନ୍ତି ଯେ ଦୁଇଜଣ ମରିଛନ୍ତି। କିନ୍ତୁ ଝିଅଟି ନିଜର ଭାବନା ଓ ସରଳତା ଅନୁସାରେ “ଆମେ ସାତଜଣ” ବୋଲି ଅଟଳ ରହେ।

⭐ Important: “’Twas throwing words away” — poetଙ୍କ repeated arguments ଝିଅଟିର ମତ ବଦଳାଇ ପାରିଲା ନାହିଁ।
ABOUT THE POET

William Wordsworth

William Wordsworth lived from 1770 to 1850.
He was one of the greatest poets of the country and of natural life.
He regarded nature as a great teacher.
His poems seek to establish an intimate relationship with nature.
He depicted simple rustic life in a number of poems.
Memorable lyrics mentioned: “Daffodils”, “The Solitary Reaper” and “Lucy Poems”.
ABOUT THE POEM

କବିତାର ମୁଖ୍ୟ ଭାବ

The poem was inspired by the poet’s meeting a child near Goodrich Castle in Wales in 1793. It begins with an image of childhood marked by simplicity, spirit and vigour, without the knowledge of death.

Eight-year-old girl→Family of seven→Two in grave→Poet says five→Girl says seven
🎯 Core understanding: The poem presents the difference between the adult’s understanding of death and the child’s innocent attachment to her dead siblings.

LET’S UNDERSTAND

Comprehension Questions

What notable features of a simple child does the poet describe in the first stanza?
What does the poet write about the habitation and the appearance of the girl?
How does the poet start the conversation with the girl and what does the girl say in reply?
What answer does the girl give to the poet relating to her brothers, sisters and mother?
Why does the answer given by the girl puzzle the poet?
What arguments does the girl put forth to prove that her two dead brothers and sisters have not been separated from her?
How does her sister suffer before her death?
How does God release Jane from her suffering?
What did she and her brother use to do round her sister’s grave when the grass was dry?
At what time of the year did John die?
How does the poem end?
LET’S APPRECIATE
