24. Quick Revision — 15 Key Points

  1. Vivekananda is presented as a great son of India.
  2. He interpreted India's religious consciousness and treasures of the past.
  3. He is described as a leader of the Indian Renaissance.
  4. His writings and speeches are strengthened by Indian scriptures and Shri Ramakrishna's life and sayings.
  5. Science and democracy are called dominant features of the age.
  6. Faith should be supported by reason and rational evidence.
  7. Religion should sustain democracy and not divide people.
  8. Life is transient and time is a powerful force.
  9. The Upanisads address the fundamental problem of the “Beyond”.
  10. The Vedas are described as accumulated spiritual laws.
  11. Yoga is personal change, adjustment and integration.
  12. Different Yogas are presented as paths to spiritual realization.
  13. Secularism includes respect for all religions and freedom of conscience.
  14. True religion is tested by truth and reconciliation with human beings.
  15. Work, service and constructive action are connected with transformation of society.
