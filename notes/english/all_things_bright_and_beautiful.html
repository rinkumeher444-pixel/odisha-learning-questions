All Things Bright and Beautiful

C. F. Alexander • Complete Classroom + YouTube Teaching Lecture

Nature→Beauty→Creation→Our Senses→Praise
🎯 Learning Objective: Understand what the poet says about the beautiful things around us, who made them, how our senses help us enjoy them, and how the poem expresses praise of God.

Beautiful things around us→Our senses experience them→We praise the beauty of creation

C. THE TEXT

Stanza 1 — Everything is Made by God

STANZA 1 • Main Idea: Creation
All things bright and beautiful,
All creatures great and small,
All things wise and wonderful,
The Lord God made them all.
Simple English: Everything that is bright, beautiful, great, small, wise and wonderful has been made by the Lord God.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଚାରିପାଖରେ ଥିବା ସୁନ୍ଦର, ଛୋଟ, ବଡ଼ ଓ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟଜନକ ସମସ୍ତ ସୃଷ୍ଟିକୁ Lord God ତିଆରି କରିଛନ୍ତି।
⭐ Important Line: “The Lord God made them all.”
C. THE TEXT

Stanza 2 — Flowers & Birds

STANZA 2 • Main Idea: Small Living Things
Each little flower that opens,
Each little bird that sings,
He made their glowing colours,
He made their tiny wings.
Simple English: God made every little flower and every little singing bird. He gave flowers their bright colours and birds their tiny wings.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଛୋଟ ଫୁଲ ଓ ଗୀତ ଗାଉଥିବା ଛୋଟ ପକ୍ଷୀକୁ God ସୃଷ୍ଟି କରିଛନ୍ତି। ଫୁଲକୁ ଉଜ୍ଜ୍ୱଳ ରଙ୍ଗ ଏବଂ ପକ୍ଷୀକୁ ଛୋଟ ପକ୍ଷ ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି।
Flower→glowing colours•Bird→tiny wings + song
C. THE TEXT

Stanza 3 — Mountain, River, Sunset & Morning

STANZA 3 • Main Idea: Natural Beauty
The purple-headed mountain,
The river running by,
The sunset and the morning,
That brightens up the sky.
Simple English: The poem describes a mountain whose top looks purple, a river flowing nearby, the sunset and the morning that brighten the sky.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ପର୍ବତର ଶୀର୍ଷ ଅସ୍ତ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟର କିରଣରେ purple ଦେଖାଯାଏ। ପାଖରେ ନଦୀ ବହେ। ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟାସ୍ତ ଓ ସକାଳ ଆକାଶକୁ ଉଜ୍ଜ୍ୱଳ କରେ।
📌 Why “purple-headed”? The glossary says the rays of the setting sun make the mountain-top appear purple, i.e. bluish red.
C. THE TEXT

Stanza 4 — Seasons & Garden

STANZA 4 • Main Idea: Seasonal Beauty
The cold wind in the winter,
The pleasant summer sun,
The ripe fruit in the garden,
He made them everyone.
Simple English: God made the cold winter wind, the pleasant summer sun and the ripe fruit in the garden.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ଶୀତ ଋତୁର ଥଣ୍ଡା ପବନ, ଗ୍ରୀଷ୍ମର ସୁଖଦ ସୂର୍ଯ୍ୟକିରଣ ଏବଂ ବଗିଚାର ପାକିଥିବା ଫଳ—ସବୁ Godଙ୍କ ସୃଷ୍ଟି।
Winter→cold wind+Summer→pleasant sun+Garden→ripe fruit
C. THE TEXT

Stanza 5 — Trees, Meadows, Rushes & Praise

STANZA 5 • Main Idea: Enjoying & Praising Creation
The tall trees in the green wood,
The meadows where we play,
The rushes by the water,
We gather every day.

He gave us eyes to see them,
And lips that we might tell
How great is God Almighty,
who has made all things well.
Simple English: We enjoy tall trees, meadows and rushes near water. God gave us eyes to see these things and lips to tell how great He is.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ସବୁଜ ଜଙ୍ଗଲର ଉଚ୍ଚ ଗଛ, ଯେଉଁ meadow ରେ ଆମେ ଖେଳୁ, ଏବଂ ପାଣି ପାଖରେ ଥିବା rushes ଆମେ ଦେଖୁ/ସଂଗ୍ରହ କରୁ। God ଆମକୁ ଆଖି ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି ଦେଖିବା ପାଇଁ ଏବଂ ଠୋଠ ଦେଇଛନ୍ତି ତାଙ୍କ ମହତ୍ତ୍ୱ କହିବା ପାଇଁ।
⭐ Most Important Lines: “He gave us eyes to see them” / “How great is God Almighty, who has made all things well.”
POEM AT A GLANCE

Five-Stanza Concept Map

1 • All creation→2 • Flowers & birds→3 • Mountain, river, sky→4 • Seasons & fruit→5 • Trees, meadows, senses & praise
Stanza 1: All things + creatures → God made them all.
Stanza 2: Flowers → glowing colours; birds → tiny wings.
Stanza 3: Mountain + river + sunset + morning → beauty of sky.
Stanza 4: Winter + summer + ripe fruit → seasonal/garden beauty.
Stanza 5: Trees + meadows + rushes → eyes see; lips tell God's greatness.
D. ABOUT THE POET

Cecil Frances Alexander

Born: Dublin, Ireland, in 1818.
She is a famous hymn writer and poet.
Famous works: ‘Hymn for Children’, ‘Christmas Carol’, ‘Once in Royal David’s City’, ‘Verses for Holy Season’.
This poem is included in ‘Hymn for Children’.

G. LET'S APPRECIATE

Think, Observe & Explain

1. Things described in the poem

Creatures: all creatures great and small.
Flowers & birds: little flower, little bird, colours, wings.
Nature: mountain, river, sunset, morning, sky.
Seasons & garden: winter wind, summer sun, ripe fruit.
Green places: tall trees, meadows, rushes by water.
Human senses: eyes and lips.

2. Which are bright and which are small?

Bright: the poem begins with “bright and beautiful”; flowers have “glowing colours”; sunset and morning brighten the sky.
Small: “all creatures great and small”; “each little flower”; “each little bird”; “tiny wings”.

3–4. Description or prayer?

The poem describes many beautiful things and also ends by expressing praise of God. The source asks students to decide whether it is a description or a prayer and explain why; the final lines address God's greatness through praise.
