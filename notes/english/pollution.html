B. THE TEXT • BHOPAL

Bhopal ଦୁର୍ଘଟଣା ଓ Airର ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱ

3 December 1984: Bhopalରେ Union Carbide ଚାଳିତ chemical plantରୁ deadly gas atmosphereକୁ escape କଲା।
Tragic result: 4000ରୁ ଅଧିକ local residents ମୃତ୍ୟୁବରଣ କଲେ ଏବଂ ଅନେକ survivor blind ଓ crippled ହେଲେ।
ମଣିଷ food ବିନା ଏକ ମାସ ଓ water ବିନା ଦୁଇ-ତିନି ଦିନ ବଞ୍ଚିପାରେ; breathing ବିନା ଏକ minute ମଧ୍ୟ ନୁହେଁ।
Average adult ପ୍ରତିଦିନ ପ୍ରାୟ 15 kg air exchange କରେ; food ପ୍ରାୟ 1.5 kg ଓ water 2.5 kg।
⭐ Exam Point: Respiration ଦ୍ୱାରା pollutants ଶରୀରକୁ ପ୍ରବେଶ କରିପାରେ।

INDUSTRIAL POLLUTION

Industries କିପରି Pollution କରେ?

Coal / Coke / Furnace oil→Burning→Heat + Smoke→Atmosphere→Lungs
Factories steam ଉତ୍ପାଦନ ପାଇଁ coal, coke, furnace oil ଭଳି fuels burn କରନ୍ତି।
Smoke ଅଦୃଶ୍ୟ ହେଲା ପରି ଲାଗିଲେ ମଧ୍ୟ atmospheric air ସହ ମିଶିଯାଏ।
Polluted airରେ obnoxious gases, ash ଓ dust particles ଥାଇପାରେ।
Thermal power stations high amounts of smoke and ash discharge କରୁଥିବା industries ମଧ୍ୟରେ first।
Cement, steel, ore processing industries ମଧ୍ୟ significant contributors।
Chemical industries toxic fumes ସହ smoke release କରିପାରନ୍ତି।
⚠️ Expression: "Our lungs slowly become garbage dumps" — pollutants lungsକୁ ପ୍ରଭାବିତ କରୁଥିବା ଚିତ୍ରଣ।
AUTOMOBILE POLLUTION

Automobile Exhaust

Buses, lorries, goods carriers, cars, jeeps ଓ two-wheelersର ବ୍ୟବହାର ବଢ଼ିଛି।
Greater Kolkataର automobiles ପ୍ରତିଦିନ ପ୍ରାୟ 1500 tonnes pollutants atmosphereକୁ ଛାଡ଼ୁଥିବା report ଦିଆଯାଇଛି।
Automobiles କେତେକ parts of the worldର air pollutionର 60% ପାଇଁ responsible ବୋଲି textରେ stated।
Automobiles maximum carbon monoxide release କରନ୍ତି ବୋଲି lessonରେ କୁହାଯାଇଛି।
🎯 Trend continue କଲେ futureରେ nasal filters ପିନ୍ଧିବା ଅବସ୍ଥା ହୋଇପାରେ।
EFFECTS

Air Pollutionର ପ୍ରଭାବ

କ୍ଷେତ୍ରପ୍ରଭାବ
🌱 CropsFlower ଓ vegetable crops car exhaust gasesର ill effects ଭୋଗନ୍ତି।
🌳 TreesPower plantsର pollution ଦ୍ୱାରା trees killed ହୋଇପାରନ୍ତି।
🐄 CattleSmeltersର fumes cattleକୁ poison କରିପାରେ।
🚗 TyresRubber tyres crack ଓ porous ହୋଇପାରେ।
🏢 BuildingsSoot wallsକୁ blacken କରେ; surfaces deteriorate ହୋଇପାରେ।
👁️ HealthEye irritation, scratchy throat, respiratory illnesses ଓ serious diseases ସହ ସମ୍ପର୍କ।
GLOBAL WARMING

Greenhouse Gases → Global Warming

Carbon dioxide+Methane+Nitrous oxide→Radiation trapped→Warmer atmosphere
📌 Scientists are concerned because these gases are increasing and tend to trap radiation reaching Earth from the Sun.
ACID RAIN

Acid Rain — କାରଣ ଓ ପ୍ରଭାବ

Oxides of sulphur + nitrogen→Precipitation→Acid rain→Environmental damage
କେଉଁଠି?କ'ଣ ହୁଏ?
Lakes & riversWater becomes acidic; aquatic plants and animals are harmed.
SoilPotassium, calcium and magnesium upper layerରୁ leach ହୋଇଯାଏ।
ForestsLarge stretches of forests die; animals and birds follow.
HealthEyes/lungs irritate; skin lesions may occur, especially in children.
BuildingsAcidic smog/rain damages old buildings and marble monuments.
📝 Example: Sweden ରେ 4000 lakes ଏତେ severely affected ହୋଇଥିଲା ଯେ no fish survived।
CONTROL

Air Pollution ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣର 3 ମୂଳ ଉପାୟ

1. Preventive

Industryର raw materials ବା fuel ingredients ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରି pollution କମାଇବା।

2. Dispersal

Smokestacksର height ବଢ଼ାଇ pollutantsକୁ disperse କରିବା।

3. Collection

Pollutants atmosphereକୁ escape କରିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ equipment ଦ୍ୱାରା trap କରିବା।
🧠 Memory: Prevent → Disperse → Collect
COUNTRIES & PROGRAMMES

Pollution Control ପାଇଁ ପଦକ୍ଷେପ

Highly industrialized countriesରେ air pollution prevent/control ପାଇଁ legislation ରହିଛି।
Wind pollutantsକୁ ଏକ countryରୁ ଅନ୍ୟ countryକୁ thousands of miles ନେଇଯାଇପାରେ।
Low-sulphur coal ଓ oil ବ୍ୟବହାର କେତେକ citiesର pollution କମାଇଛି।
Automobile engines redesign ଏବଂ catalytic converter ବ୍ୟବହାର ହୋଇଛି।
Catalytic converter pollutantsକୁ harmless substancesରେ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ କରେ।
Physicians, engineers, meteorologists, botanists research କରୁଛନ୍ତି।

KEY POINTS REVISION

15 Must-Remember Facts

1. Air pollution is a hidden menace and major future threat.
2. Bhopal accident: 3 Dec 1984; deadly gas from chemical plant.
3. Adult exchanges about 15 kg air daily; breathing essential.
4. Air: 78% N₂, 21% O₂, <1% argon, 0.03% CO₂.
5. Altered composition = polluted air.
6. Industries burn coal, coke, furnace oil → smoke/ash.
7. Thermal power, cement, steel industries major contributors.
8. Automobile exhaust is significant pollution source (60% in some areas).
9. Pollution damages crops, trees, cattle, buildings.
10. Human health: eye irritation, respiratory illnesses.
11. CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide → global warming.
12. Acid rain affects water, soil, forests, health, buildings.
13. Control: Preventive, Dispersal, Collection approaches.
14. Laws, cleaner fuels, redesigned engines combat pollution.
15. Research and large expenditure needed for lasting solutions.
