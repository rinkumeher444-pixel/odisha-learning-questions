1. Lead-in — Faith Can Move Mountains

Think First

Have you ever written a letter to God? Have you ever seen someone writing a letter to God?

Central Question

What should we put our faith in?

Story Setup

Lencho is a farmer who writes a letter to God asking for money when his crops fail.

ଓଡ଼ିଆରେ ସହଜ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା
ପାଠ ଆରମ୍ଭରେ faith ବିଷୟରେ ଭାବିବାକୁ କୁହାଯାଇଛି। Lencho ଜଣେ farmer। Crop fail ହେବା ପରେ ସେ God ଙ୍କୁ letter ଲେଖିଲେ।
📌 Remember: The story deliberately makes the reader think about faith and its power.

4. Rain Arrives — "New Coins"

Family

The older boys were working in the field; the smaller ones were playing near the house.

Rain

Big drops began to fall during the meal.

Lencho's Joy

He went outside to feel the rain on his body.

"These aren't raindrops falling from the sky, they are new coins."
⭐ Exam Point: Raindrops are compared to new coins because rain promised a good harvest.

6. One Hour of Hail — The Crop Is Destroyed

For an hour

Hail rained on the house, garden, hillside, cornfield and whole valley.

White field

The field looked as if it were covered with salt.

No leaves

Not a leaf remained on the trees.

No crop

The corn was totally destroyed and flowers were gone.

📝 Exam Point: The storm changes the story from hope for a good harvest to complete loss.

10. The Postmaster's Act of Kindness

Resolution

He decided to answer the letter so that Lencho's faith would not be shaken.

Charity

He asked employees for money, gave part of his own salary and got help from friends.

Result

He could not collect the full 100 pesos; he sent a little more than half.

⭐ Important: The postmaster's action is an "act of charity" motivated by Lencho's faith.

11. Lencho Receives the Money

Confidence

Lencho was not surprised to find money because of his complete confidence in God.

Problem

He counted the money and found that it was less than the 100 pesos he had requested.

Reaction

He became angry because he believed God could not have made a mistake.

"God could not have made a mistake, nor could he have denied Lencho what he had requested."
ଓଡ଼ିଆରେ ସହଜ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା

12. The Second Letter — The Twist

"Of the money that I asked for, only seventy pesos reached me. Send me the rest…"

Lencho's belief

He still believed the money had come from God.

His accusation

He thought the post office employees were "a bunch of crooks".

The irony

The people who helped him were exactly the people he suspected.

🎯 Most Important: The ending is built around dramatic irony: the helpers are blamed by the person they helped.
ଓଡ଼ିଆରେ ସହଜ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା
Lencho ଙ୍କ faith God ଉପରେ ଏତେ strong ଥିଲା ଯେ ସେ ଏହା ଭାବିଲେ ନାହିଁ ଯେ post office ର people ତାଙ୍କୁ help କରିଥିବେ।

17. Let's Understand the Text — Q1–Q6

1. Where did Lencho live?
He lived in the solitary house in the middle of the valley, on the crest of a low hill.
2. What did he hope for?
He hoped for rain—a downpour or at least a shower—to help the crop.
3. What did he say about the raindrops?
He said they were like new coins: big drops were ten-cent pieces and little ones were fives.
4. How did the rain change?
A strong wind began to blow and very large hailstones began to fall with the rain.
5. What happened to Lencho's corn fields?
The corn was totally destroyed and the flowers were gone.
6. Who did Lencho have faith in?
He had faith in God.

Odisha Class 10 • English

18. Let's Understand the Text — Q7–Q11

7. Who did he write a letter to?
He wrote a letter to God.
8. Who read the letter?
The postman saw it and showed it to the postmaster, who read it.
9. What did the postmaster do?
He decided to answer it, collected money from employees and friends, added part of his salary, and sent Lencho more than 70 pesos.
10. Was Lencho surprised?
No. His confidence was so strong that he showed not the slightest surprise.
11. What was Lencho's reaction after getting the letter?
He became angry after counting the money because only seventy pesos had reached him.
⭐ Important: Q10 and Q11 are often connected: no surprise → anger after counting the money.
