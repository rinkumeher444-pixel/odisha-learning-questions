1. Lead-in — The Meaning of “Goodbye”

School is ending

This is the final year in school. After the test examination, students will soon say goodbye to school.

But education continues

Leaving school means moving into the wider world and preparing to face life's challenges.

14. Let's Understand the Text — Q1–Q8

1. Who is the speaker in the essay? Who are the audience?
Speaker: the voice addressing students in the essay. Audience: pupils about to leave school.
2. What does the speaker speak about?
The speaker gives advice for future life: continuing education, choosing a profession, good health, character, leisure and citizenship.
3. What wrong notion do pupils generally have when they say goodbye to school?
They may think their education is finished when they leave school.
4. Why continue education after leaving school?
Because success in a chosen trade or profession requires continued education.
5. What care is needed in choosing a profession?
Choose with the greatest care and avoid, as far as possible, an occupation that leads nowhere.
6. What if forced to take a job you do not like?
Try to get a job to your liking as soon as possible.
7. Whose advice should you seek if unable to decide?
Advice from one of your teachers.
8. What prevents someone from achieving success?
The text stresses lack of hard work and failure to continue learning as barriers to success.

15. Let's Understand the Text — Q9–Q16

9. What is most essential for achieving success in life?
Good health is presented as a major basis of success; hard work and continued learning are also directly stressed.
10. How can you keep in good health?
Keep the body fit through cleanliness, fresh air, regular habits and suitable recreations.
11. What should we avoid to remain healthy?
Avoid anything that saps health; the text specifically warns against smoking and drink.
12. What is the priceless possession of an individual?
Character.
13. How can character be kept sound and strong?
Be truthful, considerate, fair, kind and helpful, and have courage to stand for what is good, pure and noble.
14. What should be avoided to safeguard character?
Gambling in every form should be avoided; the text also warns against bad influences.
15. How should one spend leisure hours?
Use them for interesting hobbies, good books and good companionships/associations that influence you for good.
16. How can an individual prove himself an asset to the nation?
By having a sound mind and body, good character, courteous manners and loyalty to the duties of the nation and its high ideals.

25. Grammar — Although / Though

Rule: “Although” and “though” are conjunctions used to join two contrasting statements.
Statement A
+
Contrast word
although / though
+
Statement B
Although he is leaving school, he will not forget his school days.
ସରଳ ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା
Although/Though ଦୁଇଟି contrasting statement କୁ ଯୋଡ଼େ। ଉଦାହରଣରେ “he is leaving school” ଏବଂ “he will not forget his school days” ଦୁଇଟି statement ଅଛି; although ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ contrast ଭାବେ join କରିଛି।
⚠️ Common Mistake: These words are used to show contrast; do not use them when the two statements have no contrast.

vii. Bubli has a scooty. She often goes to school on foot.
Although Bubli has a scooty, she often goes to school on foot.

28. Grammar — Imperative Sentences

Avoid

Avoid anything that will sap your health.

Make

Make provision for hard times.

Be

Be a credit to your family.

Rule: The subject “you” is usually not expressed. Imperatives can express an order, request, advice, command, warning or suggestion.
ସହଜ ଓଡ଼ିଆ ବ୍ୟାଖ୍ୟା
Imperative sentence ରେ “you” ସାଧାରଣତଃ ଲେଖାଯାଏ ନାହିଁ। ଏହା order, request, advice, command, warning କିମ୍ବା suggestion ଦେଇପାରେ। Example: “Avoid anything...” ର hidden subject ହେଉଛି “you”।
