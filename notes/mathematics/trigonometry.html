ଉଦାହରଣ — sin(90°−θ)

Given: ଗୋଟିଏ right triangle ରେ acute angle θ ଅଛି।

To Find: sin(90°−θ)।

Formula/Method: complementary angle ପାଇଁ opposite ଓ adjacent side ପରସ୍ପର ବଦଳିଯାଏ।

1
90°−θ ର opposite side = θ ର base।
2
Hypotenuse ଦୁଇ କ୍ଷେତ୍ରରେ ସମାନ।
3
sin(90°−θ) = Base/Hypotenuse।
4
Base/Hypotenuse = cosθ।
∴ sin(90°−θ)=cosθ
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: Source ର diagram ରେ ଏହି ଦୁଇ ratio ସମାନ ଦେଖାଯାଇଛି।

sec(180°−θ)=−secθ

ଉଦାହରଣ — 120° ର ratios

Given: θ=120°.

To Find: ସମସ୍ତ six ratios।

Formula/Method: 120°=180°−60°।

1
sin120°=sin(180°−60°)=sin60°=√3/2.
2
cos120°=−cos60°=−1/2.
3
tan120°=−tan60°=−√3.
4
cot120°=−1/√3.
5
sec120°=−2.
6
cosec120°=2/√3.
∴ sin120°=√3/2, cos120°=−1/2, tan120°=−√3, cot120°=−1/√3, sec120°=−2, cosec120°=2/√3.
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 120° ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ quadrant ରେ ଥିବାରୁ sin positive ଏବଂ cos/tan negative।

୮ Trigonometric Identity

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
💡
Identity ର ଅର୍ଥ ହେଉଛି—variable θ ର ଅନୁମୋଦିତ value ପାଇଁ ଯେଉଁ equality ସତ୍ୟ ରହେ। Problem solve କରିବାବେଳେ expression ରେ ଥିବା ratio ଅନୁସାରେ ସଠିକ୍ identity ବାଛିବା ଦରକାର।

ଉଦାହରଣ — sec²θ−tan²θ

Question: sec²θ−tan²θ ର ମୂଲ୍ୟ ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣୟ କର।

Given: 1+tan²θ=sec²θ.

To Find: sec²θ−tan²θ.

1
sec²θ=1+tan²θ.
2
sec²θ−tan²θ=(1+tan²θ)−tan²θ.
3
=1+tan²θ−tan²θ.
4
=1.
∴ sec²θ−tan²θ=1
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: +tan²θ ଓ −tan²θ cancel ହୋଇ 1 ରହିଲା।

୯ Compound Angles — A+B ଓ A−B

ମୂଳ ୪ଟି ସୂତ୍ର

sin(A+B)=sinA·cosB+cosA·sinB
cos(A+B)=cosA·cosB−sinA·sinB
sin(A−B)=sinA·cosB−cosA·sinB
cos(A−B)=cosA·cosB+sinA·sinB

Source pages 12–14 ରେ geometric construction ଦ୍ୱାରା four formula derive କରାଯାଇଛି। fileciteturn46file1L1361-L1424 fileciteturn46file1L1498-L1523

tan ଓ cot ର compound formula

tan(A+B)=(tanA+tanB)/(1−tanA·tanB)
tan(A−B)=(tanA−tanB)/(1+tanA·tanB)
cot(A+B)=(cotA·cotB−1)/(cotA+cotB)
cot(A−B)=(cotA·cotB+1)/(cotB−cotA)

Source page 15 ରେ algebraic derivation ସହିତ ଏହି formula ଦିଆଯାଇଛି। fileciteturn46file1L1703-L1811

ଉଦାହରଣ — sin15°

Given: 15°=45°−30°।

To Find: sin15°।

Formula: sin(A−B)=sinA·cosB−cosA·sinB.

1
sin15°=sin(45°−30°).
2
=sin45°·cos30°−cos45°·sin30°.
3
=(1/√2)(√3/2)−(1/√2)(1/2).
4
=√3/(2√2)−1/(2√2).
5
=(√3−1)/(2√2).
∴ sin15°=(√3−1)/(2√2)
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 45°−30°=15°। Source example 11 ରେ ଏହି calculation ଦିଆଯାଇଛି। fileciteturn47file1L850-L870

ଉଦାହରଣ — tan105°

Given: 105°=60°+45°।

To Find: tan105°।

1
tan105°=(tan60°+tan45°)/(1−tan60°·tan45°).
2
=(√3+1)/(1−√3).
3
Conjugate (1+√3) ଦ୍ୱାରା numerator ଓ denominator କୁ multiply କର।
4
=(√3+1)²/(1−3).
5
=(4+2√3)/(−2)=−2−√3.
∴ tan105°=−2−√3
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 105° QII ରେ, ତେଣୁ tan negative। Source example 11 ର exact result ଏହି। fileciteturn47file1L872-L900

ଉଦାହରଣ — cos70°+cos50°

Given: cos70°+cos50°।

To Find: simplified value।

1
70°=60°+10° ଏବଂ 50°=60°−10°।
2
cos70°+cos50°=cos(60°+10°)+cos(60°−10°).
3
=(cos60°cos10°−sin60°sin10°)+(cos60°cos10°+sin60°sin10°).
4
=2cos60°cos10°.
5
=2×1/2×cos10°=cos10°.
6
cos10°=sin80°.
∴ cos70°+cos50°=sin80°

ଉଦାହରଣ — tan70°·tan65°−tan70°−tan65°

ଉଦାହରଣ — A+B+C=180°

Given: A+B+C=180°।

To Prove: tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC.

1
A+B=180°−C.
2
tan(A+B)=tan(180°−C)=−tanC.
3
(tanA+tanB)/(1−tanA·tanB)=−tanC.
4
tanA+tanB=−tanC+tanA·tanB·tanC.
5
tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC.
∴ tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC

ଉଦାହରଣ — ୭୫ m ଦୂରରୁ building height

Given: BC=75 m, ∠BCA=30°, √3=1.732.

To Find: BA = building height।

Formula: tan30°=opposite/adjacent=BA/BC.

1
tan30°=BA/75.
2
1/√3=BA/75.
3
BA=75/√3.
4
BA=75√3/3=25√3.
5
BA=25×1.732=43.3 m.
∴ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର ଉଚ୍ଚତା = 43.3 ମିଟର।
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 43.3/75≈0.577, ଯାହା tan30°=1/√3 ସହିତ ମେଳାଏ। Source example 17 ର exact answer 43.3 m। fileciteturn46file2L2228-L2288

ଉଦାହରଣ — ଦୁଇ observation point

Given: AB=h, BP=a, BQ=b, ∠APB=α, ∠AQB=β ଏବଂ α+β=90°।

To Find: h ର relation।

1
tanα=h/a.
2
tanβ=h/b.
3
tan(α+β)=(tanα+tanβ)/(1−tanα·tanβ).
4
tan90°=0=(h/a+h/b)/(1−h²/ab).

h(a+b)/(ab−h²)=0.
6
ab−h²=0 ⇒ h²=ab.
7
h=√ab.
∴ AB=h=√ab

ଉଦାହରଣ — ୪୫° ଓ ୩୦° observation

Given: CD=30 m, angles 45° ଓ 30°, √3=1.732.

To Find: building height x.

1
tan45°=x/BD ⇒ BD=x.
2
tan30°=x/BC ⇒ BC=x√3.
3
BC−BD=CD ⇒ x√3−x=30.
4
x(√3−1)=30.
5
x=30/(√3−1).
6
Rationalise: x=30(√3+1)/(3−1).
7
x=15(√3+1)=15×2.732=40.98 m.
∴ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର ଉଚ୍ଚତା = 40.98 ମିଟର।

ଉଦାହରଣ — ୧୦୦ m ଓ ୩୦°, ୬୦°

Given: CD=100 m, ∠PBD=30°, ∠PBC=60°।

To Find: AB=x।

1
BQ=AB−AQ=x−100.
2
tan30°=BQ/QD ⇒ QD=(x−100)/tan30°.
3
tan60°=AB/AC ⇒ AC=x/tan60°.
4
QD=AC ⇒ (x−100)/tan30°=x/tan60°.
5
tan30°=1/√3, tan60°=√3.
6
√3(x−100)=x/√3 ⇒ 3(x−100)=x.
7
3x−300=x ⇒ 2x=300 ⇒ x=150.
∴ AB=150 ମିଟର।
Source example 21 ର exact final answer 150 m। fileciteturn47file2L1211-L1299

୧୩ Common Calculation Mistakes

  • sin, cos, tan ର numerator ଓ denominator ବଦଳାନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
  • 90°−θ ରେ sin↔cos, tan↔cot, sec↔cosec ହୁଏ।
  • 180°−θ ରେ sign ବିଶେଷ ଭାବେ ଯାଞ୍ଚ କର।
  • tan(A+B) ର denominator = 1−tanA·tanB।
  • tan(A−B) ର denominator = 1+tanA·tanB।
  • Degree symbol ° ଛାଡ଼ନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
  • Rationalisation କଲେ conjugate ସଠିକ୍ ନିଅ।
