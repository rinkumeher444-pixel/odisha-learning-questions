୧ ତ୍ରିକୋଣମିତିର ପରିଚୟ

👨‍🏫
ତ୍ରିକୋଣମିତିରେ ତ୍ରିଭୁଜର କୋଣ ଓ ତାହାର ବାହୁମାନଙ୍କ ମଧ୍ୟର ସମ୍ପର୍କ ଅଧ୍ୟୟନ କରାଯାଏ। Source ର page 1 ରେ sin θ, cos θ, tan θ, cot θ, sec θ, cosec θ ଛଅଟି trigonometric function ର ପରିଚୟ ଦିଆଯାଇଛି।
ମନେ ରଖ: ଗୋଟିଏ acute angle θ ପାଇଁ—
sin θ = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse
cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Perpendicular / Base
cot θ = Base / Perpendicular
sec θ = Hypotenuse / Base
cosec θ = Hypotenuse / Perpendicular

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧ — sin(90°−θ) କାହିଁକି cosθ?

Given: right triangle ରେ acute angle θ ଓ complementary angle 90°−θ।

To Find: sin(90°−θ)।

Method: complementary angle କୁ ନୂଆ reference angle ଭାବେ ନିଅ।

1
90°−θ ର opposite side = θ ର adjacent/base side।
2
90°−θ ର hypotenuse ସମାନ ରହେ।
3
ତେଣୁ sin(90°−θ)=Base/Hypotenuse।
4
Base/Hypotenuse = cosθ।
∴ sin(90°−θ)=cosθ
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: θ ର cosine = adjacent/base ÷ hypotenuse, ଯାହା ସମାନ ratio।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୨ — θ=120° ର ସମସ୍ତ ratio

Given: θ=120°.

To Find: sin120°, cos120°, tan120°, cot120°, sec120°, cosec120°.

Method: 120°=180°−60° ଏବଂ 60° ର standard values ବ୍ୟବହାର କର।

sin120°=sin(180°−60°)=sin60°=√3/2
cos120°=−cos60°=−1/2
tan120°=−tan60°=−√3
cot120°=−cot60°=−1/√3
sec120°=−sec60°=−2
cosec120°=cosec60°=2/√3
∴ sin120°=√3/2, cos120°=−1/2, tan120°=−√3, cot120°=−1/√3, sec120°=−2, cosec120°=2/√3.
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 120° ଦ୍ୱିତୀୟ quadrant ରେ; ସେଠାରେ sin positive ଓ cos/tan negative।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୩ — θ=135°

Given: θ=135°.

To Find: ମୁଖ୍ୟ ratios।

135°=180°−45°
sin135°=sin45°=1/√2
cos135°=−cos45°=−1/√2
tan135°=−tan45°=−1
∴ sin135°=1/√2, cos135°=−1/√2, tan135°=−1.
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: QII ରେ sin +, cos −, tan −।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୪ — θ=150°

Given: θ=150°.

Method: 150°=180°−30°.

sin150°=sin30°=1/2
cos150°=−cos30°=−√3/2
tan150°=−tan30°=−1/√3
cot150°=−√3
sec150°=−2/√3
cosec150°=2

୮ Trigonometric Identities

ମୁଖ୍ୟ ପରିଚୟ
tanθ = sinθ/cosθ
cotθ = cosθ/sinθ
secθ = 1/cosθ
cosecθ = 1/sinθ

Pythagorean Identity

sin²θ+cos²θ=1

tan identity

1+tan²θ=sec²θ

cot identity

1+cot²θ=cosec²θ

ଉଦାହରଣ ୫ — Identity ଦ୍ୱାରା expression simplify

Question: sec²θ−tan²θ ର ମୂଲ୍ୟ ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣୟ କର।

Given: 1+tan²θ=sec²θ.

To Find: sec²θ−tan²θ.

1
sec²θ=1+tan²θ ଲେଖ।
2
sec²θ−tan²θ=(1+tan²θ)−tan²θ.
3
=1+tan²θ−tan²θ.
4
=1.
∴ sec²θ−tan²θ=1
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: source exercise ର identity-based questions ରେ ଏହି ପ୍ରକାର cancellation ବ୍ୟବହୃତ ହୁଏ।

୯ Compound Angles — A+B ଓ A−B

ମୂଳ ୪ଟି ସୂତ୍ର
sin(A+B)=sinA·cosB+cosA·sinB
cos(A+B)=cosA·cosB−sinA·sinB
sin(A−B)=sinA·cosB−cosA·sinB
cos(A−B)=cosA·cosB+sinA·sinB

Source pages 13–14 ର geometric proof ରେ ଏହି four identities derive କରାଯାଇଛି। fileciteturn41file0L15-L16 fileciteturn41file0L84-L109

tan ଓ cot
tan(A+B)=(tanA+tanB)/(1−tanA·tanB)
tan(A−B)=(tanA−tanB)/(1+tanA·tanB)
cot(A+B)=(cotA·cotB−1)/(cotA+cotB)
cot(A−B)=(cotA·cotB+1)/(cotB−cotA)

ଉଦାହରଣ ୬ — sin15°

Given: 15°=45°−30°.

To Find: sin15°.

Formula: sin(A−B)=sinA·cosB−cosA·sinB.

sin15°=sin45°·cos30°−cos45°·sin30°
=(1/√2)(√3/2)−(1/√2)(1/2)
=√3/(2√2)−1/(2√2)
=(√3−1)/(2√2)
∴ sin15°=(√3−1)/(2√2)
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 15°=45°−30° ସଠିକ୍। Source page 16 ରେ sin15° କୁ 45°−30° ଭାବରେ expand କରାଯାଇଛି।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୭ — tan105°

Given: 105°=60°+45°.

To Find: tan105°.

tan105°=(tan60°+tan45°)/(1−tan60°·tan45°)
=(√3+1)/(1−√3)
=(√3+1)(1+√3)/[(1−√3)(1+√3)]
=(√3+1)²/(1−3)
=(4+2√3)/(−2)
∴ tan105°=−(2+√3)
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: 105° QII ରେ, ତେଣୁ tan negative।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୮ — cos70°+cos50°

Given: cos70°+cos50°.

To Find: ମୂଲ୍ୟକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ ratio ରେ ପ୍ରକାଶ କର।

cos70°+cos50°=cos(60°+10°)+cos(60°−10°)
=[cos60°cos10°−sin60°sin10°]+[cos60°cos10°+sin60°sin10°]
=2cos60°cos10°
=2×(1/2)cos10°
∴ cos70°+cos50°=cos10°=sin80°
Source page 17 ର example 16 ର same transformation ଦିଆଯାଇଛି। fileciteturn41file0L582-L605

ଉଦାହରଣ ୯ — tan70°·tan65°−tan70°−tan65°

Given: 70°+65°=135°.

To Find: tan70°·tan65°−tan70°−tan65°.

tan(70°+65°)=tan135°=−1
(tan70°+tan65°)/(1−tan70°tan65°)=−1
tan70°+tan65°=−1+tan70°tan65°
tan70°tan65°−tan70°−tan65°=1
∴ ମୂଲ୍ୟ = 1

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୦ — A+B+C=180°

Given: A+B+C=180°.

To Prove: tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC.

A+B=180°−C
tan(A+B)=tan(180°−C)=−tanC
(tanA+tanB)/(1−tanA·tanB)=−tanC
tanA+tanB=−tanC+tanA·tanB·tanC
tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC
∴ tanA+tanB+tanC=tanA·tanB·tanC

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୧ — 75 ମିଟର ଦୂରରୁ ଉଚ୍ଚତା

Given: ଭୂମିରେ ଦୂରତା BC=75 m, angle BCA=30°, √3=1.732.

To Find: BA, ଅର୍ଥାତ୍ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର ଉଚ୍ଚତା।

Formula/Method: tan30°=opposite/adjacent=BA/BC.

1
tan30°=BA/75.
2
1/√3=BA/75.
3
BA=75/√3.
4
BA=(75√3)/3=25√3.
5
BA=25×1.732=43.3 m.
∴ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର ଉଚ୍ଚତା = 43.3 ମିଟର।
ଯାଞ୍ଚ: tan30°=43.3/75≈0.577, ଯାହା 1/√3 ସହିତ ମେଳାଏ। Source page 21 ର example 17 ର exact numerical result 43.3 m। fileciteturn42file0L256-L290

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୨ — ଖୁଣ୍ଟର ଉଚ୍ଚତା

Given: BA=30 m, angle APB=30°.

To Find: BP.

tan30°=AB/BP
1/√3=30/BP
BP=30√3
BP=30×1.732=51.96 m
∴ BP=51.96 ମିଟର।

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୩ — ଦୁଇ observation ର angle

Given: AB=h, BP=a, BQ=b, ∠APB=α, ∠AQB=β ଏବଂ α+β=90°.

To Find: h ର relation.

tanα=h/a
tanβ=h/b
tan(α+β)=(tanα+tanβ)/(1−tanα·tanβ)
tan90°=0=(h/a+h/b)/(1−h²/ab)
h(a+b)/(ab−h²)=0
ab−h²=0
h²=ab
∴ h=√ab
Source page 22 ର example 19 ର derivation ର final result AB=h=√ab। fileciteturn42file0L387-L434

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୪ — 45° ଓ 30° ରୁ ଉଚ୍ଚତା

Given: ଦୁଇ observation point ମଧ୍ୟର distance CD=30 m; angles 45° ଓ 30°; √3=1.732.

To Find: ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର height x.

1
tan45°=x/BD ⇒ BD=x.
2
tan30°=x/BC ⇒ BC=x√3.
3
BC−BD=CD ⇒ x√3−x=30.
4
x(√3−1)=30.
5
x=30/(√3−1).
6
Rationalise: x=30(√3+1)/(3−1).
7
x=15(√3+1)=15×2.732=40.98 m.
∴ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକାର ଉଚ୍ଚତା = 40.98 ମିଟର।
Source page 22 ର example 20 ର exact result 40.98 m। fileciteturn42file0L437-L536

ଉଦାହରଣ ୧୫ — 100 m ଦୂରରେ ଦୁଇ angle 30° ଓ 60°

Given: CD=100 m, ∠PBD=30°, ∠PBC=60°.

To Find: AB=x.

1
BQ=AB−AQ=x−100.
2
tan30°=BQ/QD ⇒ QD=(x−100)/tan30°.
3
tan60°=AB/AC ⇒ AC=x/tan60°.
4
QD=AC, ତେଣୁ (x−100)/tan30°=x/tan60°.
5
tan30°=1/√3 ଓ tan60°=√3 ବସାଅ।
6
√3(x−100)=x/√3 ⇒ 3(x−100)=x.
7
3x−300=x ⇒ 2x=300 ⇒ x=150.
∴ AB=150 ମିଟର।
Source page 23 ର example 21 ର final answer 150 m। fileciteturn42file0L558-L643

୧୨ Angle of Elevation ବନାମ Angle of Depression

Elevation

ଦୃଷ୍ଟିର horizontal line ଠାରୁ ଉପରକୁ object ଦିଗରେ ଯାଉଥିବା line ସହ ଯେ angle ହୁଏ, ତାହା angle of elevation।

Depression

ଦୃଷ୍ଟିର horizontal line ଠାରୁ ତଳକୁ object ଦିଗରେ ଯାଉଥିବା line ସହ ଯେ angle ହୁଏ, ତାହା angle of depression।

Exam trick: Figure ଦେଖି ପ୍ରଥମେ horizontal line ଚିହ୍ନଟ କର। ତା’ପରେ right triangle ଚିହ୍ନଟ କରି opposite/adjacent/hypotenuse ଲେଖ। Formula ମୁଖସ୍ଥ କରିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ ratio ଚିହ୍ନଟ କର।

୧୪ Common Calculation Mistakes

  • sin, cos, tan ର numerator/denominator ବଦଳାନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
  • 90°−θ ରେ sin↔cos, tan↔cot, sec↔cosec।
  • 180°−θ ରେ sin ଓ cosec positive ରହେ; cos, tan, cot, sec ର sign negative ହୁଏ।
  • 90°+θ ରେ sign pattern ଭୁଲନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
  • tan(A+B) ର denominator 1−tanA·tanB; tan(A−B) ର denominator 1+tanA·tanB।
