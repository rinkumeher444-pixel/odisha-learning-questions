THE BEGGAR

Complete Visual Classroom Lecture

Begging→ Work→ Opportunity→ Change
Characters
Sergei • Lushkoff • Olga
Key Change
Beggar → Notary
Important Number
35 roubles per month
A. BEFORE YOU READ

ପଢ଼ିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ

1. What do you think the writer is going to tell us in the story?
2. Where do you find more beggars?
3. How do you feel when a beggar asks you for help?
4. Do you like people to beg? Why / Why not?
5. Can we have a society without beggars? Suggest some ways.
I

TEXT I — ପ୍ରଥମ ସାକ୍ଷାତ

ଭିକାରୀ Sergeiଙ୍କୁ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ ମାଗେ। ସେ କହେ ଯେ ତିନି ଦିନ ଧରି କିଛି ଖାଇନାହିଁ ଏବଂ ରହିବା ପାଇଁ ଟଙ୍କା ନାହିଁ। ସେ ନିଜକୁ village school teacher ବୋଲି କହେ।

Sergei ତାଙ୍କ ragged fawn-coloured overcoat, dull drunken eyes ଓ red cheeks ଦେଖନ୍ତି।
ସେ କହେ ଯେ Kaluga ପ୍ରଦେଶରେ ତାଙ୍କୁ ଚାକିରିର offer ମିଳିଛି, କିନ୍ତୁ ସେଠାକୁ ଯିବା ପାଇଁ ଟଙ୍କା ନାହିଁ।
Sergei ମନେ ପକାନ୍ତି ଯେ ଦୁଇ ଦିନ ପୂର୍ବେ Sadovya Streetରେ ସେ ନିଜକୁ student ବୋଲି କହିଥିଲା।
ଭିକାରୀ ସ୍ୱୀକାର କରେ ଯେ teacher ଓ student—ଦୁଇଟି କଥା fiction।
ସେ ପୂର୍ବରୁ Russian choirରେ ଗାଉଥିଲା ଏବଂ drunkenness ପାଇଁ ବାହାର କରାଯାଇଥିଲା।
Sergei କହନ୍ତି: “Work! That’s what you can do! You must work!”
Sergei ତାକୁ wood choppingର କାମ ଦିଅନ୍ତି ଏବଂ Lushkoff ରାଜି ହୁଏ।
II

TEXT II — କାଠ କାଟିବା ଓ କାମର ଆରମ୍ଭ

Lushkoff Olgaଙ୍କ ସହ wood shedକୁ ଯାଏ। ସେ ଭୋକ ପାଇଁ ନୁହେଁ, pride, shame ଓ ନିଜ କଥାରେ ଫସିଯିବାରୁ କାମକୁ ଯାଇଥିଲା।

Olga ତାକୁ wood shedକୁ ନେଇଯାଆନ୍ତି।
ତାର strength vodka ଦ୍ୱାରା ଦୁର୍ବଳ ହୋଇଥିଲା ଏବଂ ସେ କାମ କରିବାକୁ ଇଚ୍ଛୁକ ନଥିଲା।
Sergei ଜାଣ୍ଲାରୁ ଦେଖନ୍ତି ଯେ Lushkoff କାଠକୁ ଦୁର୍ବଳ ଭାବରେ axe ଦ୍ୱାରା ମାରୁଛି।
Sergeiଙ୍କ ରାଗ ହ୍ରାସ ପାଏ; ସେ ଥଣ୍ଡାରେ ଅସୁସ୍ଥ ମଣିଷକୁ କଠିନ କାମ କରାଇଥିବା ପାଇଁ ଦୁଃଖିତ ଓ ଲଜ୍ଜିତ ହୁଅନ୍ତି।
ପରେ Lushkoff snow shovel କରେ, woodshedକୁ ଠିକ୍ କରେ, rugs ଓ mattressesର dust ବାହାର କରେ।
Sergei ଅନ୍ୟ ଘରକୁ ଯିବାବେଳେ packing ଓ furniture haulingରେ ମଧ୍ୟ ତାକୁ କାମ ଦିଅନ୍ତି।
TURNING POINT

ନୂଆ କାମର ସୁଯୋଗ

Sergei ପଚାରନ୍ତି Lushkoff ଲେଖିପାରେ କି। Lushkoff କହେ, “I can.” ତା’ପରେ ତାକୁ copying work ପାଇଁ ସୁଯୋଗ ଦିଆଯାଏ।

Sergei ତାକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ letter ଜଣେ ବନ୍ଧୁଙ୍କ ପାଖକୁ ନେଇଯିବାକୁ କହନ୍ତି।
ତାକୁ copying work ଦିଆଯିବ।
Sergei କହନ୍ତି: “Work hard, don’t drink, and remember what I have said to you.”
Lushkoff ସେହି ଦିନଠାରୁ Sergeiଙ୍କ yardକୁ ଆଉ କାମ ପାଇଁ ଆସେ ନାହିଁ।
III

TEXT III — ଦୁଇ ବର୍ଷ ପରେ

ଦୁଇ ବର୍ଷ ପରେ theatreର ticket window ପାଖରେ Sergei Lushkoffକୁ ଚିହ୍ନନ୍ତି।

Lushkoff galleryର seat ପାଇଁ copper coins ଦେଉଥିଲା।
ସେ କହେ: “I am a notary now and am paid thirty-five roubles a month.”
Sergei ତାଙ୍କ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନ ଦେଖି ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଖୁସି ହୁଅନ୍ତି।
Lushkoff Sergeiଙ୍କୁ ତାଙ୍କ kind words and deeds ପାଇଁ ଧନ୍ୟବାଦ ଦିଏ।
କିନ୍ତୁ Lushkoff କହେ ଯେ strictly speaking, Olga ତାକୁ save କରିଥିଲେ।
Olga ତାକୁ scold କରୁଥିଲେ, ତା ପାଇଁ କାନ୍ଦୁଥିଲେ ଏବଂ ତାଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ କାଠ କାଟୁଥିଲେ।
ତାଙ୍କ words ଓ noble deeds Lushkoffଙ୍କ ହୃଦୟରେ change ଆଣିଲା; ସେ drinking ବନ୍ଦ କଲା।
CHARACTER MAP

ଚରିତ୍ରମାନଙ୍କ ଭୂମିକା

Sergei

Advocate → lyingକୁ ଧରିଲେ → work ଦେଲେ → repeated work ଦେଲେ → copying workର ସୁଯୋଗ ଦେଲେ।

Lushkoff

Beggar → liar → wood worker → different jobs → copying work → notary.

Olga

Cook → scolds → feels sorrow → cries → chops the wood → Lushkoffଙ୍କ ହୃଦୟରେ change ଆଣେ।

Problem→Work→Support→Inner Change→Better Life
COMPREHENSION CHECK

Textbook Questions

1. Who are the characters described in Part I? What are they doing?
2. Which country are they from? How do you know this?
3. What does the beggar look like?
4. Did Sergei get angry with the beggar? Why?
5. What made him tell lies?
6. What work did Sergei suggest?
7. Was chopping wood a punishment or help?
8. What did Sergei see from the dining-room window?
9. What other work did Lushkoff do?
10. What kind of person was Sergei?
11. Why did Sergei say his words had taken effect?
12. Did Lushkoff know reading and writing?
13. When and where did Sergei meet Lushkoff again?
14. How much salary did Lushkoff get every month?
15. Who really saved and changed the beggar’s life and how?
16. What made the beggar stop drinking?
17. Which character do you like most? Give reasons.

D. WRITING

୫୦ ଶବ୍ଦରେ ଉତ୍ତର ଲେଖ

1. Who were Sergei and Lushkoff? Describe their first meeting.
2. Did Lushkoff become a beggar by circumstances or by choice? What reasons did the beggar give to Sergei for telling lies?
3. Why did Lushkoff agree to chop wood? What were the other things Lushkoff had to do?
4. Collect information about Sergei’s cook and write a short paragraph.
5. What made the beggar change his way of life?
6. Which character do you like most and why?
7. Write a short paragraph suggesting some ways for abolishing the practice of begging in our country.
