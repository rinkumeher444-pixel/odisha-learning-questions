Delete Your Account

If you wish to delete your Odisha Learning account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

📋 Steps to Delete

  1. 1 Open the Odisha Learning app on your device.
  2. 2 Navigate to your Profile section.
  3. 3 Tap on Delete Account option.
  4. 4 Confirm your request to proceed.

🗑️ Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Name
  • Mobile Number
  • School Name
  • Quiz Progress
  • XP Points
  • Leaderboard Data

⏳ Data Retention

⚠️ Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.
