How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Odisha Learning account directly from the app.

  1. Open the Odisha Learning app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.
  5. Your account and associated data will be permanently removed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Name
Mobile Number
School Name
Quiz Progress
XP Points
Leaderboard Data

Data Retention

Deleted account data is permanently removed within 30 days of your request.
